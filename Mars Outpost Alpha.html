Mission Guide

Everything you need to keep Mars Outpost Alpha alive for 10 days!

🎮 How to Play

You are the commander of Mars Outpost Alpha. Each day (from Day 1 to Day 10) something happens on Mars or in space — a dust storm, a solar flare, or a supply decision. You pick Option A or Option B. Each choice helps one thing but hurts another, so think carefully! After you pick, click Next Day to move on. Survive all 10 Days and your crew makes it home safely. 🚀

The Golden Rule: Every choice is a trade-off. Gaining power might cost oxygen. Saving shields might hurt crew health. Plan ahead!

🛰️ Real NASA Space Weather

Every event in this game is based on real space-weather records from November 7–14, 2026, tracked by NASA's DONKI database (Space Weather Database Of Notifications, Knowledge, Information) and the WSA-ENLIL solar wind model. Those tools track solar flares, coronal mass ejections (CMEs), and geomagnetic storms in real time — the same data real mission controllers watch!

⚙️ The 4 Outpost Systems

Keep all four above 0% or the mission ends!

⚡
Power Electricity from solar panels runs everything — heaters, life support, grow lights, the landing radar, and the communications array. When power gets low, other systems start failing too.
🫧
Oxygen The habitat pumps clean air for the crew to breathe. Activities like EVA spacewalks, running machines, and sealing the bunker all use oxygen. Plants help make more — but only if the grow lights are on!
🛡️
Shields Mars has no magnetic field, so radiation storms and solar flares hit the outpost directly. Shields absorb that energy. Rovers outside, science experiments, and storms all drain shields fast.
❤️
Crew Health How well the astronauts are doing — hydration, nutrition, exercise, and rest. Zero-gravity weakens muscles, so skipping workouts or running low on water and food will hurt the whole crew quickly.

🏆 Win & Loss Rules

⚠️ Critical Warning — Below 30%
If any meter drops below 30%, a yellow alert banner flashes on screen. That system is in danger! You still have time to fix it — but act fast or it will reach zero.
💥 Mission Failed — 0%
If any meter hits 0%, the mission ends immediately. Mission Control evacuates the crew. You can always try again with a different strategy!
🌟 Mission Complete — Survive All 10 Days!
Make it through all 10 Days with every system above 0% and you earn the Junior Astronaut Certificate! Your final star rating (1–3 ⭐) depends on how healthy all four systems are at the end:
  • ⭐ — You survived! Average health above 0%
  • ⭐⭐ — Great job! Average health ≥ 50%
  • ⭐⭐⭐ — Outstanding Commander! Average health ≥ 75%
